About The Author

Hi! I’m Hannah, a 17-year-old writer from Nigeria. I’ve always loved telling stories that explore friendship, growth, and adventure. Aurelia Academy is my very first story world, and I’m thrilled to share it with readers like you.
I started writing as a way to bring my imagination to life. Over time, I realized that stories could be more than just words on a page—they can inspire, entertain, and connect people. Aurelia Academy grew from this passion, and every chapter reflects my love for storytelling.
On this website, you’ll find the complete chapters of Aurelia Academy, along with updates whenever new content is released. Expect intriguing characters, exciting plot twists, and moments that make you laugh, think, and maybe even shed a tear.
I love connecting with readers! You can reach me via email at hannahabdulmalik5@gmail.com or follow me on social media for updates and sneak peeks of upcoming stories. Your thoughts, feedback, and ideas are always welcome.
