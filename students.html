Main Characters - Auriela Academy

Amara (The Newcomer/Protagonist)

*Age: 19

*Background: From a modest middle-class family in Ibadan. Her parents are teachers who sacrificed everything for her education.

*Personality: Smart, observant, quietly confident, but cautious. She has a strong sense of justice but tends to underestimate her own strength.

*Goal: To succeed, make her family proud, and prove that she belongs in Aurelia.

*Flaws: Too trusting at first, avoids confrontation, overthinks.

*Secret: There’s a hidden connection between her scholarship sponsor and Aurelia’s dark past — one she doesn’t yet know about.

2. Zara Bello (The Glamorous Queen Bee)

*Age: 21

*Background: Daughter of a top politician. She’s beautiful, influential, and manipulative — but she hides deep insecurity beneath her perfection.

*Personality: Charismatic, sharp-tongued, confident. Knows how to use charm as a weapon.

*Goal: To protect her family’s image and maintain her control over Aurelia’s social hierarchy.

*Flaws: Arrogant, jealous, and emotionally detached.

*Secret: She knows more about the “disappearance” of a former student than she lets on

3. Tega Williams (The Mysterious Popular Guy)

*Age: 22

*Background: Comes from old money. Everyone admires him, but no one really knows him.

*Personality: Calm, charming, intelligent, but distant — with that “mysterious male lead” energy.

*Goal: To escape the shadow of his father, a corrupt business magnate.

*Flaws: Secretive, emotionally guarded, and manipulative when necessary.

*Secret: He’s connected to a secret society on campus — one that might be tied to the academy’s dark past.

4. Ifeoma “Iffy” Okonkwo (The Loyal Best Friend)

*Age: 20

*Background: A tech-savvy student from Enugu, also on scholarship.

*Personality: Funny, outspoken, protective, the emotional backbone of Amara’s world.

*Goal: To make it big in tech and pull her family out of poverty.

*Flaws: Impulsive, overly loyal, can get herself into trouble.

*Secret: She accidentally uncovers a piece of information that puts her in danger.

5. Damilola “Dami” Alade (The Rival-Turned-Friend)

*Age: 21

*Background: Middle-class, but pretends to be rich to fit in.

*Personality: Bold, sassy, competitive — hates Amara at first for stealing the spotlight.

*Goal: To prove she’s not inferior to the elites.

*Flaws: Pride, jealousy, insecurity.

*Secret: She’s hiding the real reason she was admitted into Aurelia — and it’s not academic.

6. Professor Nathaniel Grey (The Charismatic Lecturer)

*Age: 35

*Background: A British-Nigerian academic with charm and mystery. He’s young, brilliant, and the students’ favorite lecturer — but whispers say he’s too close to some of the elites.

*Personality: Intelligent, composed, persuasive, but secretive.

*Goal: To protect a secret linked to Aurelia’s founding.

*Flaws: Manipulative, emotionally detached.

*Secret: He’s connected to the missing student case — and knows what really happened.

7. The Phantom Circle (Secret Society / Hidden Antagonists)

*Description: A secret group formed by elite students and alumni. They pull strings behind the scenes — controlling grades, social events, and even scandals.

*Goal: To protect the academy’s reputation and power — at any cost.

*Known Members: Unknown, but rumors point to Zara, Tega, and some top-level professors.
