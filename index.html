Welcome to Auriela Academy

At first glance, Aurelia Academy shines – marble floors, bright uniforms, and students destined for greatness. But beneath the flawless surface lie unspoken fears, broken dreams, and quiet rebellions.

Every student here has a story - of amibition, identity, and the battle to stay true to themselves. Some rise, some fall. But all of them learn what it truly means to shine from within.

Synopsis

Hidden behind golden gates and polished walls, Aurelia Academy is Nigeria’s most elite school. When scholarship student Amara steps through its doors, she discovers a world of privilege, mystery, and danger. Friendships are tested, rivals clash, and buried secrets threaten to destroy everything. As she uncovers the truth about Project Aurelia, Amara must decide: fight, flee, or become part of the system herself. Perfection has a price. At Aurelia, everyone pays it.
